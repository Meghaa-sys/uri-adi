Traditional Kerala Festival Game

MĀYĀLI — Uri Adi VR

The decorated clay pot swings from the frame! Pick up your wooden stick with your Meta Quest controller, time your swing, and break the pot!

🥽 Meta Quest / WebXR Controls:
  • Press "Enter VR" button at bottom right on Quest Browser.
  • Move controller to stick & squeeze trigger/click to Grab Stick.
  • Swing your controller to hit the swinging pot!
🖱️ Desktop / Mobile: Click on stick to pick up, then click pot to swing!
difficulty: medium
